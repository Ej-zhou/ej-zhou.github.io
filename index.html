About

I am doing my third year of a PhD at the Language Technology Lab, University of Cambridge, advised by Prof. Anna Korhonen. I am working with the Qwen Pretrain/Multilingual team as a research intern at the moment.

I work on multilingual NLP: getting LLMs to work well, and fairly, across languages. My interests span interpretability, alignment, generalisation, tokenisation, bias and calibration.
